## Step 1: Create Your Account

<Steps>
<Step title="Visit Tooli">
Go to your organization's Tooli portal or main signup page.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose Sign-Up Method">
Select from:
- Email and password
- Google account
- Microsoft account
- Buildwise SSO (if available)
</Step>

<Step title="Fill Your Details">
- Full name
- Display name (how others see you)
- Professional email address
- Password (8+ characters with letters and numbers)
</Step>

<Step title="Verify Email">
Check your inbox for verification link (valid for 7 days).
</Step>
</Steps>

### Membership Verification
- **If whitelisted:** Immediate access to all features
- **If not found:** Option to request manual review
- **If invited:** Accept invitation link to join existing organization

## Step 4: Your First Conversation

### Choose the Right Agent
<Tabs>
<Tab title="General Questions">

- Material properties and specifications
- Basic calculations and measurements
- Safety requirements and procedures
- General construction advice

**Example:** "What are the thermal requirements for exterior walls in Belgium?"
</Tab>

<Tab title="Document Work">
**Use specialized agents for:**
- **Quote Builder** - Client requests

## Step 5: Working with Documents

### Upload Your First File
<Steps>
<Step title="Click Attachment">
Look for the paperclip or upload icon in the chat input area.
</Step>

<Step title="Select File">
Choose PDF, DOC, PPT, TXT, IMG, or Excel (up to 10MB, max 10 files).
</Step>

<Step title="Wait for Processing">
Tooli will extract text and prepare the document for analysis.
</Step>

<Step title="Ask Questions">
Ask specific questions about the content.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Document Tips
<CardGroup cols={2}>
<Card title="Be Specific" icon="target">
**Good:** "What does section 3.2 say about waterproofing?"  
**Avoid:** "Tell me about this document"
</Card>

<Card title="Use Clear Files" icon="eye">
**Best:** Text-based PDFs and clear scans  
**Avoid:** Blurry images or corrupted files
</Card>
</CardGroup>

## Getting Better Results

### Question Best Practices

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Good Questions">
✅ "What are the Belgian fire exit requirements for office buildings?"  
✅ "Review this insulation specification for compliance"  
✅ "Calculate concrete volume for 20m x 15m foundation"  
✅ "What PPE is required for working above 3 meters?"
</Tab>

<Tab title="Avoid These">
❌ "Tell me everything about construction"  
❌ "What should I do?" (without context)  
❌ "Fix my project" (too vague)  
❌ "Make me rich" (not construction-related)
</Tab>
</Tabs>

### When to Use Which Mode
<CardGroup cols={2}>
<Card title="Fast Mode Examples" icon="zap">
- "What's the standard rebar spacing?"
- "List required PPE for concrete work"
- "Convert 15 cubic meters to tons of concrete"
- "What's the minimum ceiling height for offices?"
</Card>

<Card title="Thinking Mode Examples" icon="brain">
- "Analyze this full project for code compliance"
- "Review this 50-page specification thoroughly"
- "Assess all risks in this construction method"
- "Compare three different foundation approaches"
</Card>
</CardGroup>

## Common First Questions

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Is my data secure?" icon="shield">
Yes - 100% EU hosting, GDPR compliant, encrypted storage, no data used for AI training.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I delete conversations?" icon="trash">
Yes - Delete individual chats anytime, or they auto-delete after 12 months of inactivity.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if the answer is wrong?" icon="alert">
Always verify critical information. Tooli provides sources and disclaimers. For legal advice, consult your organization or Buildwise.be.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I share conversations?" icon="share">
Yes - Create secure, private share links that aren't indexed by search engines.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How much does it cost?" icon="dollar-sign">
Access is included with your partner organization membership. Usage limits may apply.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use it offline?" icon="wifi">
No - Tooli requires internet connection for AI processing and up-to-date information.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
